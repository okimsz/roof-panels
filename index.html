SHELTER / MODEL NOTES

Slow the heat. Keep the house.

Insulation resists conductive heat transfer. Increase the thickness or choose a lower-conductivity material to reduce heat flowing through the roof. In “Roof cutaway”, one section lifts to reveal the outer steel skin, insulation core, and inner liner while the house stays in place.

Three controls, one experiment

Change outdoor temperature (25–45 °C), panel thickness (50–300 mm), and core material. Cyan heat-flow markers show heat crossing the roof; orange rays represent sunlight. Flow reverses when the roof loses heat. Marker motion is illustrative, not air or refrigerant flow.

What the numbers mean

Core resistance R = d/k. Overall U = 1/(Routside + Rskins + Rcore + Rinside). Roof heat gain Q = U A (Tsol-air − Troom). Negative Q means heat leaves the house. Heat-flux reduction compares both roofs at the same temperature difference; it is not a whole-house energy saving.

Nominal roof area is 54.4 m² (6 × 8 m footprint with a 1.6 m ridge rise, excluding overhangs). Two 0.5 mm steel skins use k = 50 W/(m·K). Surface resistances are 0.04 and 0.10 m²·K/W. Representative dry core conductivities: polyurethane 0.025, EPS 0.036, mineral wool 0.040 W/(m·K). Real products vary with density, moisture, temperature and ageing.

Solar absorption is 0.55 and external heat transfer coefficient is 25 W/(m²·K), giving Tsol-air = Toutside + 0.55 G/25. Long-wave sky exchange and roof thermal storage are omitted. Indoor temperature follows C dT/dt = Qroof + 70(Toutside − Troom) + 180 W, with effective thermal capacity C = 3 MJ/K. The orange comparison uses the same model without core insulation.

This is an educational lumped model, not a building compliance calculation. There is no HVAC, detailed weather, air leakage model, thermal bridging, humidity or moisture simulation. The roof section is lifted for visibility, but inspection does not change the thermal model. With constant heating and no cooling, insulation slows heat entry; it does not maintain a fixed indoor temperature.
